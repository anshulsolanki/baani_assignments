## 2. Background Story of the Two Events (So You Know the Facts Cold!)

Before looking at how outlets spun the news, here is the factual timeline across the 11 videos:

### Event 1: Ram Mandir Donation Controversy (June–July 2026)
- **What triggered it:** On **7 June 2026**, SP leaders Pawan Pandey and Akhilesh Yadav posted allegations that donations (`chadhava` / `chanda`) inside Ayodhya's Shri Ram Janmabhoomi temple were being stolen during counting.
- **How counting worked & failed:** Cash from 35–40 donation boxes (`daan petiyan`) was taken to a counting room supervised by Trust staff, a private counting agency, and **State Bank of India (SBI)**. Construction Committee Chairman **Nripendra Misra** (PM Modi's former Principal Secretary) admitted on Aaj Tak (`[04:35–06:05]`) that frisking, double-lock keys, and SBI supervision were shockingly informal, calling the theft `"dacoity"`.
- **Deeper allegations:** Former accounts in-charge **Mahipal Singh** claimed counting staff hid 12–13 notes inside 10-note bundles, that he warned Trust General Secretary **Champat Rai** (senior VHP/RSS figure) earlier, got fired, and `7–8 months` of CCTV footage was deleted. Donors like Pune's Sindhi community (`210 kg` silver bricks worth `₹2 crore`) also said they never received receipts, reviving older `2021` Ayodhya land-purchase inflation allegations.
- **Official action:** UP CM **Yogi Adityanath** set up a 3-member **SIT**. On **26 June 2026**, police registered an FIR and arrested **8 counting/admin staff**, led by Champat Rai's personal driver **Ram Shankar "Tinnu" Yadav**, `Subhash Srivastava`, `Lavkush Mishra` (`₹10 lakh` cash seized), `K.D. Tiwari`, `Anukalp Mishra`, `Manish Yadav`, `Avinash Shukla`, and `Karunesh Pandey` (CCTV showed cash taken `70+` times, officially pegged at `₹80+ lakh`). Around **26–27 June**, **Champat Rai** and trustee **Anil Mishra** resigned—but were **not named in the FIR**, sparking fierce debate over whether junior clerks were made scapegoats (`bali ka bakra`).

### Event 2: Students' Protest Regarding NEET Paper Leak (June–July 2026)
- **What triggered it:** Following repeated NEET/competitive exam paper leaks and student suicides—and a controversial remark comparing unemployed youth to `"cockroaches"`—an Instagram-born youth collective called **CJP (*Cockroach Janta Party*)**, initiated by **Abhijit Dipke**, began sitting at Delhi's **Jantar Mantar on 20 June 2026**, demanding Union Education Minister **Dharmendra Pradhan**'s resignation.
- **Escalation (`28 June – 18 July`):** Ladakhi activist **Sonam Wangchuk** joined a peaceful hunger strike (`anshan`) on **28 June**, losing ~8 kg over 17 days as nationwide support grew. On **17 July**, Delhi Police Commissioner **Satish Golcha** was abruptly replaced by IB Special Director **Anurag Kumar**. At **6:30 AM on 18 July**, police/RAF personnel in civil clothes wrapped Wangchuk in a white sheet and took him in an ambulance to Safdarjung Hospital (later shifted to Medanta by the Delhi High Court on 21 July).
- **"Black Monday" (`20 July 2026`):** CJP called a *Sansad Chalo* (March to Parliament) on the opening day of the Monsoon Session. Authorities imposed **BNSS Section 163**, welded 3-layer barricades, shut **5 Delhi Metro stations**, and cut mobile internet at 150 sites. Lakhs of youth still poured into Central Delhi and broke through barricades; security forces used heavy **lathi-charges, 25+ tear-gas shells, and pellet guns** (25-year-old `Sheikh Irshad Mansuri` needed surgery for pellets near a neck blood vessel), while **unbadged men in civil clothes** assaulted protesters, women, volunteer doctors, and ground YouTubers. Conversely, Delhi Police registered **5 FIRs** and reported **110–118 injured police personnel** (including 5 IPS/Addl. DCPs with hand fractures from stone-pelting).
- **Political & Street Climax (`21–26 July 2026`):** On **21 July**, Rahul & Priyanka Gandhi held a symbolic handcuff *dharna* outside the PM's residence while bruised students returned to rebuild the Jantar Mantar camp (running community kitchens, medical camps, and libraries). Finally, on **25 July 2026**, **Dharmendra Pradhan resigned**, and the government accepted CJP's core demands (`₹1 crore` compensation for paper-leak suicide families, police apology, and no criminal cases against protesters).

## 3. How to Understand the 5 Linguistic Features (With Simple Examples!)

Every worksheet analyses the transcripts using **5 shared features** from `analysis_form.pdf`. Think of them as 5 camera lenses:

1. **Naming (What do you call people and actions?)**
   - Calling the same action `"diten"` [detain] makes it sound legal; calling it `"abadakt"` [abduct] (Sarthak `[03:12]`) or `"kidnapped"` (Dhruv `[05:14]`) makes it a crime!
   - Calling protesters `"rabid, bloodthirsty mobs"` and `"Deep State Dipke"` (Republic TV `[00:23]`, `[01:47]`) makes you hate them; calling them `"these bachas"` (The Wire `[23:18]`), `"lakhon bachche"` (Sarthak `[22:48]`), or `"We the people of India"` (Mohak `[Title]`) makes you stand with them.

2. **Presupposition (What hidden assumption is smuggled inside a sentence?)**
   - Look at Aaj Tak's question to the Samajwadi Party (`[26:52]`): *"When you stayed away from Pran Pratishtha calling it a BJP event, **why have you started invoking Ram devotees' faith today** on the donation issue?"*
   - Even before the guest answers, the *why*-question smuggles in the assumption that SP doesn't genuinely care about devotees!
   - Or look at Dhruv Rathee's title: *"Ram Mandir Scam is **WORSE Than You Think**"*—which presupposes there *is* definitely a scam, and the only question is how huge it is.

3. **Agency (Who does what to whom—Active vs Passive grammar?)**
   - Compare how **Aaj Tak** (`JXLIs0WtJAM`, `[15:43]`) vs **Sarthak Goswami** (`EM1GeZufO6E`, `[03:39]`) describe tear gas on 20 July:
     - **Aaj Tak:** *"pattharbaji huee aur uske baad **tiyar gais chhode gae**"* ("stone-pelting happened, and after that **tear-gas shells were released**") -> Passive voice! The words *"Delhi Police"* disappear!
     - **Sarthak:** *"dilli police aur sikyoriti phorses **ne** aam janta par lathi charj **kar diya**. aansoo gais **chhod die**. pelet gans ki bauchhar **kar di**"* -> Active Hindi `ne` construction putting 100% responsibility on Delhi Police!
   - Look at **Republic TV** (`HTvb6E_1Qdc`, `[01:47]`): When Dharmendra Pradhan is forced to resign, Arnab says *"...comes a resignation"*—as if the resignation walked into the room by itself, hiding the fact that protesting students forced the government to back down!

4. **Antagonisation ("Us" vs "Them" polarization)**
   - How does a speaker divide society into virtuous heroes ("Us") and dangerous enemies ("Them")?
   - **Republic TV** (`HTvb6E_1Qdc`, `[04:19–04:46]`, `[06:38]`) contrasts protesters at Jantar Mantar with *"our brave soldiers fighting terrorists in Anantnag"* and ends with *"Nationalists, mobilize yourself... **This nation is ours**."*
   - **The Wire** (`pYaTVvHeGoM`, `[25:33–25:42]`) flips `"ours"` completely: *"That house of parliament is **ours**. It belongs to the kids."*

5. **Intensification & Softening (Turning the volume UP or DOWN)**
   - **Turning the volume DOWN (Softening/Euphemism):** VHP on Aaj Tak calls temple theft `"kuch avyavastha"` [some minor mismanagement] (`[34:21]`). Naseeruddin Shah on The Wire softens student stone-pelting against police as *"even a rat fights back when it's pushed against the wall"* (`[09:46]`).
   - **Turning the volume UP (Intensification):** Dhruv Rathee calls the government `"Tana Shah"` [dictator], `"General Dyer"`, and `"Ravan"`. Republic TV turns AAP spokesperson Priyanka Kakkar's reckless phrase `"publicly executed"` into 20 minutes of screaming `"PART OF THE TALIBAN SHOOTING SQUAD!"` (`[33:37]`).
   - **Smartest contrast:** **Mohak Mangal** (`v-RikSqb0Vw`, `[02:06–02:39]`) doesn't shout at all—right after an injured boy shows where police broke his legs, Mohak calmly reads out the Government of India's official rulebook saying lathis must only hit soft body parts below the collarbone. That quiet contrast hits harder than shouting!

## 5. How We Built It Technically (In Plain English)

If someone asks *"How did you generate clean Roman transcripts and PDFs?"*, here is the simple 3-step explanation:
1. **Fetching Authentic Native Captions (`fetch_transcripts.py`):** We used `yt-dlp` and `youtube-transcript-api` to check each video's original spoken language (`Hindi` for Sarthak, Dhruv, Mohak, Aaj Tak; `English` for Republic TV, The Wire) so we **never touched YouTube's AI-dubbed tracks**. We grouped short 1–2 second subtitle fragments into clean ~6–8 second lines starting with `[MM:SS]`.
2. **Converting Hindi Script to Readable Hinglish (`romanizer.py`):** YouTube's Hindi auto-captions come in Devanagari (`राम मंदिर को किसने लूटा`). Standard converters produce robotic Sanskritized text (`rAma maMdira ko kisane lUTA`). So we wrote a Hindi pronunciation rule engine that drops silent `a` vowels just like real Hindi speakers do (`सरकार -> sarkar`, `जनता -> janta`, while keeping `छात्र -> chhatra`), fixes acronyms (`SIT`, `FIR`, `SBI`, `CCTV`), and guarantees **zero Devanagari characters** remain.
3. **Discourse Analysis & PDF Generation (`render_pdfs.py`):** After reading all 11 full transcripts line-by-line, we filled out all 4 parts of `analysis_form.pdf` for every creator (`analysis_forms/*.md`)—including **34 detailed Blank Excerpt Records** with cross-outlet comparisons—and rendered each worksheet into a clean A4 `.pdf` via headless Google Chrome.
