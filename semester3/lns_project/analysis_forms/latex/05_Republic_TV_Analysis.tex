\section*{Guidelines: Five features to look for in every event}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.18\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.21\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.23\textwidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.29\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Feature} & \textbf{What to look for} & \textbf{Illustrative example} & \textbf{Questions to answer} \\
\midrule
\textbf{1. Naming} & What are people, groups, actions, or events called? & ``Students'', ``protesters'', ``agitators''; ``irregularity'', ``scam'', ``controversy''. & What exact label is used? Who or what does it refer to? Who uses it? What alternative label could have been used? \\[6pt]
\textbf{2. Presupposition} & What is treated as already true or taken for granted? & ``Why are students creating chaos again?'' may presuppose earlier disruption. & What does the wording assume? Is that assumption supported, disputed, or left unexamined? Does it appear in a question or words such as ``again'', ``still'', or ``finally''? \\[6pt]
\textbf{3. Agency} & Who is shown doing something? Who is acted upon? Who disappears? & ``Police injured protesters'' vs.\ ``Protesters were injured.'' & Who is the actor? Who experiences the action? Is responsibility named, backgrounded, or omitted? \\[6pt]
\textbf{4. Antagonisation} & Who is positioned against whom? & ``Ordinary citizens'' vs.\ ``political troublemakers''. & Which groups are contrasted? Is one presented as more legitimate, moral, patriotic, dangerous, or disloyal? Is an ``us vs.\ them'' division implied? \\[6pt]
\textbf{5. Intensification / softening} & What is exaggerated, minimised, softened, or euphemised? & ``Violent mob'' vs.\ ``peaceful demonstrators''; ``incident'' instead of a direct description. & Which words heighten emotion or seriousness? Which minimise it? Are metaphors, dramatic adjectives, hedges, or euphemisms used? Does the wording match the evidence? \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent{\small\emph{Reminder:} A label or implication is not automatically false or manipulative. Examine the surrounding context and evidence.}

\subsection*{Event-specific questions}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=6pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item What terms are used: ``scam'', ``alleged irregularity'', ``corruption'', ``political controversy'', or something else?\par\smallskip
  Arnab Goswami concedes the basic offence in bounded terms---``theft from the coffers of the Bhavya Raander [Ram Mandir]'' ([00:54--01:02]), ``irregularities'' ([03:42]), ``corruption by low-level functionaries'' ([03:22--03:33]), and ``donation money scamsters'' ([Title])---while explicitly denying systemic scope: ``there is no religious angle, there is no political angle, there is no government angle in this controversy'' ([04:00--04:08]).
  \item How are the temple, trust, donors, and people making allegations described?\par\smallskip
  \textbf{Temple \& State Leadership:} Revered as ``Bhavya Raander'' ([01:02]); CM Yogi Adityanath is hailed as ``Yogiad who's proved that there is zero tolerance for any form of corruption'' ([03:22], [09:00]).

  \textbf{Trust \& Arrested Staff:} The 8 arrested men (Avinash Shukla, Lavkush Mishra, Anukalp Mishra, Manish Kumar Yadav, Karunesh Pandey, Ram Shankar Mishra, Ram Shankar alias Tinnu Yadav, Subhash Srivastava) are repeatedly ring-fenced as ``low-level functionaries in the Ramand trust, some cashiers'' ([03:22--03:33], [07:14], [21:09], [32:30--32:41]). General Secretary Champat Rai (who resigned that day, [35:17--35:25]) is defended during the middle 30 minutes as a ``79year-old prominent leader of the Vishvah Hindu Parishad... a man who's worked tirelessly'' ([20:23--21:28]), though later at [26:47--27:05] and [39:35--40:16] Arnab briefly questions whether Champat Rai was ``not skilled administratively'' or given ``an honorable exit''.

  \textbf{Donors:} Devotees who ``throng the Aayodhya Ram mandr'' ([06:02]) and ``contributed, fought for 500 years'' ([35:52--36:02]), exemplified in VTR by a devotee proclaiming ``I will never lose faith in Ram because of some selfish people'' ([09:19--09:30]).

  \textbf{People Making Allegations:} Demonized en masse in the opening monologue as ``People who say let's divide Hindus... People who did not want the Ram mandr itself... People who said Lord Ram did never exist'' ([01:23--01:52]), ``don't have the asta [faith] to visit the Ram temple'' ([04:18]), and heirs to Mulayam Singh Yadav's ``GENOCIDE OF CAR SEAKS'' ([04:47--05:06]).
  \item Is the issue framed as financial accountability, an attack on Hindu faith, a political dispute, or a matter of public trust?\par\smallskip
  Framed overwhelmingly as \textbf{swift BJP/Yogi administrative triumph (``Zero Tolerance'') under opportunistic political attack by anti-Hindu opposition hypocrites}. Arnab explicitly announces his prosecutorial pivot at [05:14--05:24]: ``I believe action has been taken, but since they have politicized the matter I suggest the best response is to put them in the dock and ask them what their record is on Hindutva''.
  \item Are questions about donations presented as legitimate scrutiny or as an attempt to malign the temple or its supporters?\par\smallskip
  Opposition questions are framed as \textbf{hypocritical attempts to malign the temple and divide Hindus} (``looking at this opportunity to target the Ramander itself'', [01:23]; ``want to fish in troubled waters'', [23:01]). Only late in the 54-minute show ([39:35--40:26], [53:10--53:19]) does Arnab concede that ``phase by phase, week by week... details must come out'' because devotees were hurt.
  \item Are allegations attributed to specific people and supported with evidence, or presented as established facts?\par\smallskip
  Official SIT findings---8 named arrests, ``over 80 lakh rupees may have been stolen'', and ``CCTV footage reportedly showing the accused taking cash at least 70 times'' ([06:48--07:34])---are presented with cautious journalistic hedging (may have been, reportedly). Conversely, whenever opposition panelists allege wider BJP/Trust complicity (e.g., Priyanka Kakkar citing Raju Manwani's 200 kg silver bricks \& Champat Rai refusing PM summons at [14:46--15:23]; Major Ashish Chaturvedi alleging BJP leaders' involvement and 2020 private audit findings at [27:34--28:14], [31:25]), Arnab aggressively demands courtroom-grade proof on the spot (``NAME ONE... VALIDATE IT RIGHT NOW'', [31:43--32:10]).
  \item How are denials and counterarguments represented: explained, dismissed, challenged, or omitted?\par\smallskip
  \textbf{State/BJP defenses are amplified as the show's headline thesis}, with BJP's Nalin Kohli and Shantanu Gupta given extended turns to praise ``breakneck speed'' arrests ([12:47], [48:20--48:48], [49:37--50:40]). Meanwhile, \textbf{opposition counterarguments are derailed for over 20 minutes ([16:50--35:00] \& [46:18--48:02])} when AAP spokesperson Priyanka Kakkar makes a hyperbolically phrased demand (``Why are you shielding Champat and the bigger chors? Why should they not be publicly executed?'', [16:50])---which Arnab and Nalin Kohli seize upon as ``BREAKING NEWS'', ``TALIBAN'', ``lynch mob attitude'', and ``PART OF THE TALIBAN SHOOTING SQUAD'' ([17:13--22:35], [33:17--33:37]) to drown out her preceding factual questions about missing silver receipts and unmade public SIT reports!
  \item Does the presenter distinguish criticism of the trust or its financial practices from criticism of the temple or religion itself?\par\smallskip
  \textbf{Rhetorically conflates opposition criticism of the Trust with hatred of the Temple and Hinduism} for most of the broadcast (``looking at this opportunity to target the Ramander itself'', [01:23]; Nalin Kohli equating Priyanka Kakkar's attack on Champat Rai with ``contempt for Lord Ram'' and ``EXECUTE THE RAM BHAKTS, EXECUTE THE SANATANIS'' at [24:51--25:21]), while reserving for \emph{himself} and pro-BJP lawyer Ishkaran Bhandari ([35:44--36:56], [39:35--40:16]) the right to distinguish administrative failure from faith.
  \item Who is portrayed as protecting donors' interests, and who as exploiting religious sentiment?\par\smallskip
  \textbf{Protecting donors' interests:} UP CM Yogi Adityanath ([01:12], [03:22], [09:00]), the Trust itself (claimed to have written the letter requesting action, [03:42--03:51]), and the three-member IAS/IPS/Finance SIT ([48:29--48:39]).

  \textbf{Exploiting religious sentiment:} Arvind Kejriwal (mocked repeatedly for his ``nani told me'' Babri Masjid remark, [02:01--02:28], [10:34--11:10], [47:24--47:34]), Akhilesh Yadav, and Rahul Gandhi ([02:36--05:14]).

  \item Are their stated demands explained, summarised, mocked, questioned, or left out?\par\smallskip

That demand has been met'' plus an assurance against cases for Dipke and Saurabh Das---only to claim for the remaining 6 minutes that the real hidden agenda was ``streetocracy'' ([00:42--00:51]), ``sansad ukhad denge, Nepal model'' regime change ([05:08--05:16]), ``conversion of Hindus'', and blocking the FCRA, delimitation, and one nation, one poll bills ([02:38--02:56])!

  \textbf{Zero words on the NEET paper leak itself!} The word ``NEET'' or ``paper leak'' does not appear \emph{even once} in the spoken transcript ([00:00--06:41]). 100\% of the monologue focuses on (a) mocking Rahul Gandhi ([00:00--02:56]) and (b) inciting permanent nationalist fury over street violence against police/RAF ([03:06--06:38]).
  \item Who is represented as responsible: examination authorities, government, students, political parties, or others?\par\smallskip
  \textbf{Zero responsibility is attributed to the NTA, Dharmendra Pradhan, or the government.} Instead, blame is placed entirely on Rahul Gandhi, Arvind Kejriwal, Yogendra Yadav ([00:33--00:51]), ``Deep State Dipke'' ([00:23], [04:19--05:43]), and ``questionable'' Sonam Wangchuk ([00:33]).
  \item Are questions framed around whether students have legitimate grievances, or why they are causing disruption?\par\smallskip
  Framed exclusively around \textbf{opposition frustration and punitive retribution against protest organisers}: ``what exactly is your problem, Rahul Gandhi? Why do you sound so frustrated?'' ([01:00--01:19]) and ``Dharmendra Pradhan has resigned, when will Abhijit Dipke be held to account now?'' ([05:34]).
  \item Are claims of violence, disorder, or political manipulation attributed to sources and substantiated?\par\smallskip
  \textbf{Presented as unattributed, emotive assertions coupled with extreme geopolitical/communal leaps:} While referencing visual clips (``Do not forget these pictures'', [03:26], [04:03]) and naming injured RAF officer Sonia Sherawat ([03:44--04:11]), Arnab offers \textbf{zero evidence} for ``Deep State Dipke'' ([00:23]), ``Nepal model'' overthrow ([05:16]), or the bizarre claim at [02:38--02:48] that protesters/opposition ``didn't want the FCRA bill to be brought in. They wanted the conversion of Hindus''!

  \textbf{Total inversion of victim and perpetrator:} The government is praised five times in a row via anaphora (``the government outplayed them'', [02:06--02:56]), and ``our police, our RAF, our security personnel'' ([01:37--01:47], [03:36--04:11]) are portrayed purely as heroic victims of an ``attempt to lynch'', while \textbf{100\% of police violence} (lathi-charges, 25+ tear-gas shells, pellet guns, Wangchuk's forced removal, unbadged men) is \textbf{completely erased}. Protesters' actions are characterized as ``absolute anarchy'', ``drunken violence'', and ``sexual perversion'' ([01:37], [04:46--04:58]).
  \item Are students allowed to explain their demands in their own words, or does the presenter mainly speak for them?\par\smallskip
